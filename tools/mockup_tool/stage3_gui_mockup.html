Selected windows

Each lane is a min/max overview of the same signal shown in that modality's section above — the ventilator lanes follow whichever channels are ticked there. Drag a handle to resize a window, drag its band to move it, or click + Keep window to select another range — click a window (on the canvas or in the list) to edit it with the Start/End fields above. ⤢ Whole signal stretches the keep window across the entire recording (collapsing any others into it), for when you want all of it — on its own that saves the recording unchanged, and combined with remove windows it means "everything except those spans". − Remove window adds an inverse (hatched red) span: that stretch is cut out and everything around it is kept — use it to drop a coughing artefact or a disconnect out of the middle of a recording. With only removed spans and no keep windows, the whole recording minus those spans is saved. Any row's keep/remove chip flips it between the two. Save as new sequence concatenates the resulting segments, in time order, into one named sequence — the same effect as chaining eit.slice + Sequence.concatenate() — and adds it to the persistent Saved sequences library below.

Saved sequences
