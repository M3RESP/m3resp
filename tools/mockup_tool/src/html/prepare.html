Raw synchronizationThe ventilator is the reference. The offset of a recording, in seconds, is where its first sample sits on the ventilator time line. The bar of each row shows it.Signal is the signal of the row; Compared with is the ventilator signal it is matched to. EMG signals are compared as their envelope (RMS over 0.3 s, ECG removed).An empty offset field uses the estimated offset: the cross-correlation of the two signals, over −5.0 to +5.0 s. A change of signal gives a new estimate.Type a value to replace the estimate. With the method estimate_offset, Estimate finds the offsets of all rows again and clears the typed values.Match is the correlation at the offset in use (1 is the same shape).session.sync_raw applies the offsets.

Use it to remove a cough or a disconnection from the middle of a recording.Whole signal makes one keep window over the whole recording and joins the other keep windows into it.
