EMG signals

Each EMG signal is one channel of the loaded Poly5 file, labelled with the muscle it records from. All signals share one time axis, so they line up below each other. Channel 1 (EMGdi) is the diaphragm signal that emg.preprocess and emg.ecg_gating work on by default. The raw traces still carry ECG — that is what emg.ecg_gating removes before emg.detect_breaths runs.

Selected windows

Each lane is a min/max overview of the same signal shown in that modality's section above — the ventilator lanes follow whichever channels are ticked there. Drag a handle to resize a window, drag its band to move it, or click Keep window to select another range — click a window (on the canvas or in the list) to edit it with the Start/End fields above. Whole signal stretches the keep window across the entire recording (collapsing any others into it), for when you want all of it — on its own that saves the recording unchanged, and combined with remove windows it means "everything except those spans". Remove window adds an inverse (hatched red) span: that stretch is cut out and everything around it is kept — use it to drop a coughing artefact or a disconnect out of the middle of a recording. With only removed spans and no keep windows, the whole recording minus those spans is saved. Any row's keep/remove chip flips it between the two. Save as new sequence concatenates the resulting segments, in time order, into one named sequence — the same effect as chaining eit.slice + Sequence.concatenate() — and adds it to Saved sequences (under the Save bar and in the side panel).
